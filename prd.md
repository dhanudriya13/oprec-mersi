

# PRD — MERSI SIFORS UNDIKSHA Website

**Product:** MERSI SIFORS UNDIKSHA
**Product Type:** Student PR & Recruitment Website
**Primary Goal:** Introduce MERSI and recruit students into the MERSI PR team
**Target Audience:** Current Information Systems students, especially prospective/new students and students interested in joining the PR team
**Primary CTA:** Apply for MERSI
**Application Platform:** External recruitment form/link
**Status:** Initial Product Specification
**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Product Overview

## 1.1 Background

MERSI SIFORS UNDIKSHA is a student PR/creative team associated with the **Information Systems Study Programme (SIFORS)**.

The team supports the study programme's communication and student recruitment activities through creative content, visual communication, and quality assurance.

MERSI consists of three primary teams:

1. **Copywriting**
2. **Creative Design**
3. **QA/QC**

The website will serve as the official digital landing page for MERSI, introducing its identity, activities, team structure, recruitment opportunities, and application process.

---

# 2. Product Goals

The website should accomplish five primary goals.

### G1 — Introduce MERSI

Visitors should quickly understand:

* What MERSI is
* What MERSI does
* Why MERSI exists
* Its relationship with SIFORS Undiksha

### G2 — Attract Students

The website should make students interested in becoming part of MERSI.

### G3 — Explain Available Roles

Students should clearly understand the three teams:

* Copywriting
* Creative Design
* QA/QC

### G4 — Convert Visitors Into Applicants

The website should make applying extremely easy.

The primary conversion should be:

> **Visitor → Understand MERSI → Interested → Apply**

### G5 — Establish a Professional Digital Identity

The website should make MERSI feel like a serious, organized, modern student organization rather than simply a social-media committee.

# 13. Recruitment Status

The website should support at least three recruitment states.

### OPEN

Display:

> 🟢 OPEN RECRUITMENT

And show:

> **APPLY NOW**

### UPCOMING

Display:

> 🟡 COMING SOON

Disable or replace the application button.

### CLOSED

Display:

> ⚪ RECRUITMENT CLOSED

Show:

> Applications for this recruitment period are currently closed.

This prevents visitors from applying to an outdated recruitment form.

# 36. Recommended Design Principle

The most important principle for the AI agent:

> **Design the website as a recruitment experience, not an organizational information page.**

The visitor should feel:

```text
"What is MERSI?"
       ↓
"Oh, that's interesting."
       ↓
"I could actually contribute here."
       ↓
"I want to join."
       ↓
"Where do I apply?"
       ↓
```

That should drive the entire UX.
